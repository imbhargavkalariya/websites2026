Process

Our 4-Step Export Workflow

Structured execution ensuring regulatory precision and timely international delivery.

01

Requirement & COA Review

Client submits technical specification requirements. We review pharmacopeial grades (IP, USP, EP) and sample COAs.

02

Commercial Terms & Dossier

Finalize proforma invoice, payment terms, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and initiate CTD dossier package preparation.

03

Batch Testing & Packaging

Goods undergo final analytical quality clearance and are secured in export HDPE fiber drums or moisture-proof blister packs.

04

Customs Clearance & Freight

Dispatch via air/sea freight with authenticated customs paperwork, temperature logger tracking, and airway bill notification.

FAQ

Capabilities & Technical FAQ

What regulatory documentation is provided with API orders? ▼
All commercial API orders (such as IP/USP Etomidate API Powder and Mitomycin API Powder) are accompanied by a comprehensive Certificate of Analysis (COA), Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS), Stability Data summary, and CTD Module 3 technical documentation.
How are temperature-sensitive APIs handled during export shipping? ▼
Sensitive raw materials requiring cold-chain preservation (e.g., refrigerated 2°C–8°C storage) are packaged in validated insulated thermal shippers with temperature data loggers to monitor thermal integrity throughout international air transit.
Can Jani Pharma assist with country-specific drug registration filings? ▼
Yes. Our Regulatory Affairs team, headed by Dr. Sanjay Patel, provides CTD and eCTD dossier formatting, technical query responses, and documentation alignment for Ministry of Health registrations worldwide.
