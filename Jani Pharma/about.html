Corporate Background

About Jani Pharma

Delivering international quality assurance, pharmaceutical regulatory compliance, and dependable global export sourcing.

Our Journey

A Commitment to Pharmaceutical Quality & Precision

Established in Surat, Gujarat, Jani Pharma has grown into a key exporter and sourcing partner for high-purity Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), specialty chemical intermediates, and finished dosage oral formulations.

We operate at the intersection of stringent pharmacopeial standards and global trade logistics. By partnering exclusively with WHO-GMP compliant manufacturers and maintaining rigorous batch testing protocols, we guarantee batch consistency and full document traceability.

Headquarters Location Surat, Gujarat, India
Quality Alignment WHO-GMP & ISO 9001:2015

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Corporate Mission

To empower international pharmaceutical markets by delivering certified raw materials and finished formulations with absolute purity, total regulatory transparency, and punctual worldwide dispatch.

👁️ Global Vision

To be recognized across international healthcare sectors as the premier sourcing gateway for pharmaceutical APIs, CTD dossier support, and reliable cold-chain supply.

Principles

Guiding Corporate Values

Our core values shape every client interaction, regulatory submission, and batch clearance.

1. Regulatory Rigor

We enforce strict compliance with pharmacopeial standards (IP, USP, EP) and WHO-GMP guidelines, providing full analytical verification for all shipments.

2. Supply Chain Integrity

From ambient warehouse storage to specialized temperature-controlled air freight, we maintain strict cold-chain logistics protocols.

3. Transparent Partnership

We build long-term international trade relationships based on clear communication, custom dossier support, and fair commercial terms.
